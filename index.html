How it's madeOne HTML file, no models or images. A 400² heightfield with baked horizon occlusion, ~4,500 instanced canopy blobs, merged procedural architecture, and a GPU wave equation for ripples. The scene renders to a half-float target; water is shaded afterwards with depth-based Beer–Lambert absorption, refraction, Fresnel sky and screen-space reflections, then ACES tone mapping and a light tilt-shift.
